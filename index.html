About Levinah

A unisex studio built on care and craft

Levinah Unisex Salon & Makeup Studio is a full-service beauty destination in Bunamwaya, Sseguku, Wakiso District, welcoming women, men and kids for hair, makeup, nails, barbering and bridal styling.

Every service is delivered by professional experts using quality products in a clean, relaxing environment — and every price is built to stay affordable without cutting corners.

Beyond the chair, Levinah also runs certified training programs for people who want to build a career in beauty, hairdressing, makeup and more.
